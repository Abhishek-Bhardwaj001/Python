A paragraph may not follow when its sentences lack unity, coherence, or a clear controlling idea, causing the text to feel disjointed or confusing.
Understanding Paragraph Structure
A paragraph is a group of sentences that focuses on a single main idea, often introduced by a topic sentence and supported by evidence, examples, or explanations. Each sentence should relate directly to the controlling idea, and the paragraph should conclude with a sentence that wraps up or transitions to the next idea
